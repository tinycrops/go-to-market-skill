What one run leaves behind

Everything around the product that makes it purchasable

A public page on Modal

HTTPS URL, GPUs on demand, nothing served from your home network, nothing billed while idle.

Free trial, then credit packs

No accounts or passwords. A browser key holds credits; Stripe Checkout sells packs with inline prices, so there's no dashboard setup.

A checkout proven safely

verify.py opens a real session, confirms an unpaid claim is refused, then expires the session so nobody can pay it.

Live stats

Visitors, runs, checkouts, sales and feedback written to STATS.md every 30 minutes.

A summary video

At most 45 seconds, cut from the real terminal output and the live page, autoplaying at the top of the product page.

A verdict and a launch kit

What it beats, what it loses on, who it is good enough for today, plus draft posts that wait for your go-ahead.

The procedure in SKILL.md

Ten steps, mostly mechanical

  1. Orient

    Check what Stripe has actually seen. Research real customer needs and free alternatives into NEEDS.md before choosing the offer.

    5 min

  2. Scaffold

    new_product.py <slug> "<Name>" creates the storefront, metering, stats and the Modal secret. The stub deploys as-is.

    2 min

  3. Wire the product

    Fill estimate_cost, run and the packs. Charge in units the customer sees; debit before the work and refund on failure.

  4. Design around the customer's decision

    Write DESIGN.md first: audience, primary task, real proof, phone behaviour. Every claim must trace to a measurement.

  5. Deploy and verify

    modal deploy web.py, then verify.py. Browser acceptance at 390 px and 1440 px, recorded in DESIGN-QA.md.

  6. Measure and give a verdict

    Measure what the page promises on real inputs, compare against the best alternative, and write the readiness verdict.

  7. Summary video at the top

    summary_video.py cuts a 45-second-max clip from the captured terminal output and the live page, and the page opens with it. Like the one above.

  8. Stats, demo, launch kit

    Stats timer, an optional cut with sound for X, and draft posts.

  9. Hand off and record

    A handoff note, a memory entry, a commit.

  10. Ask, then stop

    Live URL, the verdict in two lines, the demo link, and one question, such as “post it?”

references/stripe.md

Payment with one restricted key and no webhook

The key only needs Checkout Sessions read and write. Sales are confirmed by reading the session back when Stripe redirects the buyer.

1 · KEY

The browser holds a random access key. No accounts.

2 · CHECKOUT

The server opens a session tagged with the key, product and pack.

3 · CLAIM

On redirect the server reads the session and credits once, only if it's paid and for this product.

4 · RESTORE

The receipt's session id restores the key on another device.

Hard boundaries

What the skill will not do

Serve from the house

Home serving lagged the operator's games. Everything public runs on Modal.

Post or contact anyone

No posts, DMs, emails or directory listings without a yes. Drafts stay drafts.

Print an unmeasured number

Every figure on a page is measured and recorded in the product's CLAUDE.md.

Store undisclosed data

The privacy text must say exactly what the code stores.

Pay a live session to test

verify.py creates one and expires it.

What you need

  • Claude Code, with the repo cloned into ~/.claude/skills/go-to-market.
  • A Modal account and the modal CLI.
  • A Stripe restricted key with only Checkout Sessions: read + write.
  • Python 3.10+ with requests.

Where it stands

Written on 2026-10-08. Its first run took a voice product to a verified live storefront. The author then chose not to sell it on quality grounds, which is the decision the readiness verdict exists for. Paths in SKILL.md come from the author's machines; change them to match yours.
